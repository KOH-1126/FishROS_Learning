Autumn arrives on a quiet golden breeze.  
The summer sun grows softer every day.  
Leaves turn from green to amber in the trees.  
A gentle wind carries their colors away.  
The morning air feels cool against my face.  
Thin clouds wander slowly through the sky.  
Birds gather together before leaving this place.  
Their distant voices fade as they fly.  
The forest wears a coat of red and gold.  
Small paths disappear beneath fallen leaves.  
The evenings come earlier and feel more cold.  
A lonely branch quietly bends and grieves.  
Sunlight dances softly across the ground.  
Dry leaves whisper whenever I walk.  
There is a peaceful music in their sound.  
The trees seem to listen whenever I talk.  
Apples hang heavy in the orchard rows.  
Pumpkins rest beneath the fading sun.  
A cool river through the countryside flows.  
The busy days of summer are almost done.  
Smoke rises slowly from a distant fire.  
Its gentle scent drifts through the evening air.  
The sunset burns like a quiet desire.  
For one brief moment, gold is everywhere.  
The moon appears above the empty field.  
Stars shine brighter in the colder night.  
The earth seems ready for its dreams to yield.  
While windows glow with warm and yellow light.  
I hear the wind moving through the trees.  
It carries stories from another year.  
Some memories return with every breeze.  
Some disappear before they become clear.  
Autumn teaches us how things can change.  
Beauty can live even when moments end.  
The world becomes familiar and strange.  
Each falling leaf feels like a silent friend.  
I watch them turning slowly in the air.  
They do not fight the wind that makes them fall.  
They simply drift without a thought or care.  
And find their resting places after all.  
Soon winter will cover every tree.  
The fields will lose their autumn shades of gold.  
But for a while, the season stays with me.  
A quiet story waiting to be told.  
So I walk beneath the fading afternoon.  
I breathe the cool and gentle autumn air.  
The sun is sinking, and the night comes soon.  
Yet something warm is still remaining there.  
Autumn leaves, but not without a trace.  
Its golden memory stays in every place.
